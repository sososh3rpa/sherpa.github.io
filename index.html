About Us

Guardians of Sikkim's
Sherpa Legacy

For over five decades, the Sherpa Global Association has been the cultural backbone of the Sherpa community in Sikkim. We work to preserve our unique language, promote traditional arts and festivals, and support the wellbeing of Sherpa families across the region.

From documenting oral histories to organizing the annual Pang Lhabsol festival, our work ensures that the rich Sherpa heritage — the language, the songs, the stories, the values — continues to thrive in the modern world.
